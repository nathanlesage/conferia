# Your Personal Agenda

Next to the search field, you can find a checkbox that, when clicked, will only
show items on your personal agenda. Use this to only view events you intend to
visit.

Your personal agenda is meant for you to put together a list of sessions,
keynotes, and other events that you wish to participate in.

## Adding Events to your Personal Agenda

To add an event to your personal agenda, you simply click the little bookmark
icon in the bottom-right of either the event card, or the event details dialog.
By clicking the icon multiple times, you toggle whether the event is part of
your personal agenda.

Once an item is on your personal agenda, it will receive an outline to indicate
to you that it is part of your agenda and distinguish it from other events.

> [!warning]
> Conference schedules are typically subject to last-minute changes, even as the
> conference already happens. Conferia.js uses a heuristic to match any new
> changes to events that are already on your personal agenda. This means that,
> if presentations are added, removed, and swapped in individual sessions, those
> sessions will remain on your agenda and reflect these changes. However, if
> too much information about an event changes, Conferia.js will no longer be
> able to associate the changes with any event on your agenda, meaning that the
> event will no longer be part of your agenda.
>
> This is why we recommend that you confirm your personal agenda shortly before
> the conference starts.
